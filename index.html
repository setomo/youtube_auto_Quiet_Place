Quiet Place

A personal tool for publishing videos to its operator's own YouTube channel.

This is not a product and has no users other than its operator. It is a set of command-line scripts run on one personal computer. There is no hosted service, no sign-up, and no way for anyone else to connect an account to it.

What it does

It assembles short documentary videos about travel destinations in Japan and uploads them to a YouTube channel owned by the person running it. The steps are:

  • Research places from public reference sources
  • Collect openly-licensed photographs, with their licence and credit recorded
  • Generate narration and render a video locally
  • Upload it to the operator's own channel, with captions

Why it uses Google APIs

Only to publish to the operator's own channel. The scopes requested, and what each is for:

  • youtube.upload — upload the finished video
  • youtube — set its title, description and privacy, and add it to a playlist
  • youtube.force-ssl — attach the caption track (captions.insert requires this specifically)
  • yt-analytics.readonly — read view counts for the operator's own videos

Who can use it

One person: the operator, on their own machine, against their own channel. The OAuth client exists so that person can authorise their own account. It is not offered to anyone else.
